# CodexLink use notes

CodexLink is an unofficial Alpha/developer-preview client, provided under the [ISC license](../LICENSE), including its warranty disclaimer. Third-party notices remain applicable to their respective components.

Build and operate it on devices and projects you are authorized to access. You are responsible for Codex account usage, network configuration, signing, backups, and decisions to approve tool execution. Preserve your existing sandbox and approval policy.

Remote operations can modify files or run commands on your Mac. Stop interrupts a Turn and may not terminate already-backgrounded tools. Network failures and notification delays are possible; inspect authoritative session state before repeating an uncertain operation.
